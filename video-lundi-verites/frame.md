# Design spec — DigitalMikaelson ad

Locked brand truth for this composition. Matches the "Gagne ta vie sans
diplôme" ebook cover exactly — this video and the ebook must read as the
same brand.

## Palette

- **Background:** `#211E5C` (navy) — solid fill, diagonal light/dark navy
  gradient variant for the open/CTA scenes only. No full-screen linear
  gradient on any scene that holds text more than 2s (H.264 banding) — use a
  radial glow or a solid + localized glow instead.
- **Foreground:** `#FFFFFF` (white text on navy).
- **Accent 1 (primary):** `#F7C72A` (yellow) — headline emphasis words, key
  lines, dividers.
- **Accent 2 (secondary):** `#7EDEE8` (cyan) — small pills/badges only, never
  large fields.
- **Black:** `#121212` — CTA bar only (matches the ebook's cover CTA bar).

## Type

- **Headlines / on-screen text:** Bricolage Grotesque, 700–900 weight.
- **Secondary / labels / captions:** WorkSans, 400 (body) and 700 (bold
  labels).
- Two typefaces total. No third font.

## Do

- Real assets only: the ebook's actual cover art, the real WhatsApp icon,
  the real photos already generated for this campaign.
- Rounded pill badges (brand tag, category tags) — same radius language as
  the ebook.
- Kinetic word-by-word or line-by-line text reveals for punch lines.
- Thin accent lines/arcs as structural decoration (already used in the
  ebook's schema graphics).
- CTA bar identical in spirit to the ebook cover: black bar, real WhatsApp
  icon, phone number, no price.

## Don't

- No gradient text (`background-clip: text`).
- No neon/glow overuse — cyan stays a small accent, never a wash.
- No stock-photo clichés beyond the 4 already-approved campaign photos.
- No price, no invented statistic, no invented testimonial, no invented
  date/deadline.
- No second display font.
